ABOUT TGT DISCOVERY ACADEMY

TGT Discovery Academy / NPC (Reg: K2025221297) • Established 21 July 2022 in Roseville, Pretoria, South Africa.

OUR BACKGROUND & PURPOSE

TGT Discovery Academy is a community-oriented soccer development academy established on 21 July 2022. Our primary objective is to identify, nurture and develop young football talent, particularly within underserved communities, townships and surrounding areas.

We provide young boys and girls with opportunities to discover their potential, improve their football abilities and develop into confident, disciplined and responsible individuals. At TGT Discovery Academy, we believe that talent exists everywhere—our responsibility is to discover it, develop it and create opportunities for it to shine.

OUR VISION & MISSION

Our Vision: To develop talented, confident and disciplined footballers who can compete at the highest levels while becoming responsible individuals and future leaders both on and off the field.

Our Mission: To provide accessible, structured and quality football development programs that focus on technical ability, physical fitness, mental strength and personal development—instilling discipline, teamwork, respect, commitment and a passion for football.

OUR 7 CORE VALUES

  • Discipline: Encouraging commitment, consistency and accountability.
  • Respect: Respecting teammates, coaches, opponents and officials.
  • Teamwork: Building unity and a strong team spirit.
  • Integrity: Promoting honesty, fairness and good sportsmanship.
  • Commitment: Remaining dedicated to individual and team goals.
  • Inclusivity: Creating opportunities for young players from different backgrounds.
  • Excellence: Striving for continuous improvement and achievement.

COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT

TGT Discovery Academy believes that football is more than just a game. It is an opportunity to change lives, build confidence and create positive futures.

Through football, we encourage healthy lifestyles, positive behavior, discipline, education and personal growth—contributing toward stronger communities by developing talented footballers and responsible future leaders.

PLAYER DEVELOPMENT PROGRAMME (AGES 8–21)

Holistic football training for boys and girls between the ages of 8 and 21 years—from beginners to competitive league athletes.

1. TECHNICAL DEVELOPMENT

  • Ball control and coordination
  • Passing and receiving
  • Shooting and finishing
  • Individual football skills
  • Creativity and confidence on the ball

2. PHYSICAL DEVELOPMENT

  • Physical fitness and conditioning
  • Speed and agility
  • Strength and coordination
  • Overall physical well-being

3. MENTAL DEVELOPMENT

  • Confidence and mental toughness
  • Concentration and discipline
  • Resilience and determination
  • Positive attitudes and a growth mindset

4. SOCIAL & PERSONAL DEVELOPMENT

  • Teamwork and communication
  • Leadership development
  • Respect and sportsmanship
  • Responsibility and commitment

AGE GROUPS & ELIGIBILITY (8 TO 21 YEARS)

TGT Discovery Academy caters for boys and girls between the ages of 8 and 21 years. We welcome beginner and intermediate players interested in building their football fundamentals, as well as ambitious young athletes preparing for competitive league play and higher-level scouting opportunities. Our philosophy prioritizes player development while recognizing the importance of competition and winning.
